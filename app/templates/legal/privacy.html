This page explains what personal data Unfurl stores, why, how long, and what you can do about it.

{{ h2("Who is responsible") }}

Unfurl is run by {{ operator.name }} ({{ operator.address }}), the controller under the GDPR. Contact: {{ operator.email }}. Unfurl does not need a data protection officer; write to this address for anything about your data.

{{ h2("What we store and why") }}
  • Your account: name, email address, a hash of your password (never the password itself), your time zone and settings.
  • What you save in the app: your profile (experience, education, skills, languages, certifications, projects, optional photo, date of birth and nationality), your CVs and their versions, the jobs you save and their notes, your learning goals and tasks, and your notifications.
  • Why: to provide the service you signed up for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Without an account and the data you enter, the app can't work; everything beyond your name, email and password is up to you.
  • Ticking "I agree" when you sign up confirms that you read this page; it is not a consent we rely on.
{{ h2("Data about other people") }}

Job ads you save can contain a recruiter's name or email address. We store them only because you saved them, to help you with your application (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)), and they are deleted with the job or your account.

{{ h2("Security and abuse protection") }}

To stop password guessing and spam, the app counts requests and failed sign-ins per IP address and email address for up to one hour. These counts are kept only in memory and are never written to a database (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)). The hosting provider keeps technical logs with IP addresses for a short time; details follow before launch.

{{ h2("Who processes data for us") }}
  • Hosting and database in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany). [Provider named before launch.]
  • Email delivery for verification, password and reminder emails. [Provider named before launch.]
  • Each provider works under a data processing agreement (Art. 28). AI features are not active yet; this page will list the AI provider before they are.
{{ h2("No automated decisions") }}

Unfurl makes no decisions about you with legal or similar effect (Art. 22). Suggestions in the app only help you; you decide what to do.

{{ h2("How long we keep it") }}
  • Everything in your account: until you delete it, or delete your account.
  • Notifications: read ones 30 days, unread ones 90 days.
  • A deleted item you can still undo: a few minutes.
  • When you delete your account, all of it is deleted at once, including your photo.
{{ h2("Your rights") }}
  • Access and portability: Settings › Privacy › "Export my data" downloads everything as one file.
  • Erasure: Settings › Privacy › "Delete my account" deletes everything for good.
  • Rectification: you can change your data in the app at any time.
  • Restriction and objection: write to {{ operator.email }}.
  • Complaint: you can complain to a data protection authority, in Austria the Datenschutzbehörde (dsb.gv.at).
{{ h2("Browser extension") }}

The Unfurl extension for Chrome works only when you click it, and only on the tab you have open. When you open it, it sends the page's link to Unfurl to check whether you saved that job before; Unfurl doesn't keep this link. It reads the page's title and text (and the job details some sites add for search engines), and sends them to Unfurl only when you save the job. On a karriere.at search page, it also reads the selected job's own page on karriere.at once, without your cookies; Unfurl receives the same kind of data as from any job page. It never reads your cookies. When you sign in to it, it stores a token in your browser; we keep only a hash of the token. Tokens end after 90 days, when you sign out, or when you revoke them in Settings › Browser extension.

{{ h2("Cookies") }}

Only what the app needs: a session cookie while you are signed in, and a cookie with your theme choice (light or dark). No analytics, no advertising, so no cookie banner.

{{ h2("Minimum age") }}

Unfurl is for people aged {{ minimum_age }} or older.

{{ h2("What is never sent to AI") }}

Your date of birth, nationality and photo are optional and are never sent to an AI provider.
