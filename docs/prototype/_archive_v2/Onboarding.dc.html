Save jobs while you browse

The Unfurl extension adds a button to Chrome. When you look at a job on LinkedIn, StepStone, karriere.at or any career page, one click saves it to your applications.

  1. 1Install"Add to Chrome" opens the Chrome Web Store page of Unfurl. Click "Add extension" there.
  2. 2Pin itClick the puzzle icon 🧩 next to the address bar, then the pin next to Unfurl. The flag icon stays visible.
  3. 3Save a jobOn a job posting, click the flag icon. Check the details and press Save. See the popup
