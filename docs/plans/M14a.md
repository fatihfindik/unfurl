## 5. Hazır set (İngilizce, D-178)
**Likely questions** (kategori → soru):
- General: "Tell me about yourself." · "Why are you looking for a new role?" · "Where do you see yourself in a few years?"
- Behavioural: "Tell me about a time you solved a difficult problem." · "Tell me about a mistake you made and what you learned." · "Describe a conflict in a team and how you handled it." · "Tell me about a time you had to learn something quickly."
- Role: "What are your strongest skills for this role?" · "What would you do in your first 90 days?" · "What are your salary expectations?"
- Company: "Why do you want to work at {company}?" · "What do you know about {company}?"

**Questions to ask them** (Soru 4'e bağlı): "What does success look like in the first 90 days?" · "What does a typical week look like for the team?" · "What are the biggest challenges for this role right now?" · "What are the next steps in the process?"
